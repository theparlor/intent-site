Coherence Engineering · a new discipline for AI-augmented teams

Agile was a coordination tax.
What makes coordination a compounding investment?

One person with AI agents now produces at the pace of five people three years ago. The frictions agile was built to solve are disappearing. But companies are cutting teams to capture the savings — and destroying the mechanism that produced the one thing AI can't generate: the clarity to know what should exist.

The reframe

Agile's ceremonies evaporate after each sprint. You pay the coordination tax and never get it back. But what if coordination produced artifacts that made the next cycle better? Research findings, executable specs, verified contracts, validated design decisions — those compound. That's coordination as investment, not tax.

The growth arc problem

Programmer
Learned syntax, executed known patterns
Developer
Composed patterns to solve new problems
Engineer
Applied CS principles to frame problems
Architect
Read and designed systems from experience
Each stage forced internalization of patterns through friction. The friction was the teacher.

AI-assisted coding lets a junior skip the friction and produce senior-looking output. But the output wasn't the point — the compression was. You learned to read architecture because you'd built things that failed. You communicated about pattern choices because you'd lived through the consequences of wrong ones.

Now you can't smell their code to see where someone needs to grow. And companies will use AI productivity gains to eliminate teams — destroying the mechanism that produced the deep domain understanding that makes AI-assisted work actually valuable.

Coherence at every altitude

Agile got stuck at the team level. SAFe "solved" scaling by stacking ceremonies on ceremonies until the coordination tax crushed the thing it was supposed to enable. Coherence engineering avoids this through a single principle: coherence scales through artifact composition, not ceremony multiplication.

Intent: the reference implementation

Coherence engineering is the discipline. Intent is how you practice it. A continuous loop that produces compounding artifacts instead of evaporating ceremonies.

Notice

Capture signals

Friction, questions, observations, anomalies from anywhere work happens. Every signal is atomic and trust-scored.

Spec

Shape contracts

Transform signals into agent-ready specifications with verifiable contracts. Specs are the coordination medium.

Execute

Agents build

Agents implement against contracts. Trust-gated at L0–L4 autonomy. Contracts pass or fail with evidence.

Observe

Loop closure

Runtime feedback becomes new signals. The artifacts produced in this cycle make the next cycle better.

How it enters your organization

Coherence engineering doesn't replace your quarterly reviews, your program management, or your financial governance. It runs underneath them and feeds them. Your quarterly review still happens — but now the presenter has artifact-derived inputs instead of hand-built narratives.

The VP still makes decisions — but now there's a structural signal confirming or contradicting the gut. Nobody has to change their identity. They just get better inputs to the practice they already trust.
