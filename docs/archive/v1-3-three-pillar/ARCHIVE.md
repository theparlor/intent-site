# Archive — v1.3 three-pillar snapshot

**Frozen:** 2026-06-05
**Trigger:** Phase 10 worldview overhaul (DEC-004 — C-minimal narrowing + dogfood-evidence organizing principle)
**Tag:** `intent-site-pre-overhaul-2026-06-05` (exact bytes, on origin)
**Reason:** Retire the three-pillar navigation era — preserve a recoverable, readable snapshot

## What this is

This is the intent-site state on 2026-06-05, immediately before the Phase-10 overhaul. 23 HTML pages representing the **v1.3 "three-pillar" era** of the site — the IA that organized everything under three primary-nav links: **The Story** (`pitch.html`), **The System** (`work-system.html`), **The Build** (`architecture.html`).

This era was itself the post-2026-04-09 subtraction rebuild (see `../v1-2-multi-framing/ARCHIVE.md`). It successfully killed the multi-framing problem, but by 2026-06 the site had drifted into a NEW collision: three navigation eras superimposed in one `docs/` folder —

1. the live three-pillar pages (these),
2. a half-built four-zone refresh in `docs/v2-draft/` that was never promoted, and
3. the v1.2 archive.

Every three-pillar page also carried a `v1-banner` that punted visitors into `v2-draft/pitch.html` — so a visitor was snapped between two different information architectures mid-session.

## What replaced it

The **four-zone IA** (DEC-004), promoted from `docs/v2-draft/` to `docs/` root on 2026-06-05:

- **The Hypothesis** (`pitch.html`) — single committed target user (staff+ engineers on teams of 2–7 using Claude Code daily), Intent framed as an active research hypothesis, the competitive alternative named (Dunford gate).
- **The System** (`the-system.html`) — methodology-layer hub linking the depth pages.
- **The Build** (`the-build.html`) — architecture hub linking the depth pages.
- **The Proof** (`the-proof.html`) — the dogfood-evidence ledger: "Intent governs N real repos" as evidence, never a 21-product catalog.

**Important — this is an evolve-in-place overhaul, not a teardown.** The rich depth pages (work-system, personas, signals, schemas, architecture, agents, decisions, …) were **kept live at root** and re-navved to the single four-zone navigation; the four zone-hubs link to them. This snapshot is the *historical record of the three-pillar nav era*, not the only surviving copy of that content. The depth pages continue forward under the new IA.

## Why archived rather than deleted

1. **Recovery point.** If the new IA performs worse on a future panel review, roll back to this baseline (also captured exactly by the git tag).
2. **Historical record.** Git history is hard to browse; a readable subfolder snapshot makes the era's nav and framing inspectable at a glance.
3. **Honest accountability.** The site documents its own evolution; archiving rather than deleting shows what each era actually shipped.

## What's NOT in this snapshot

- `review-2026-04-09.html` — stays live as the forcing-function artifact (re-navved to the new IA)
- `index.html` — stays live (the redirect)
- `styles.css` — shared stylesheet, stays live
- `visual-brief.html` and `visual-brief-app/` — not part of the IA
- `.meta.yml` sidecars — library-index enrichment files, regenerated by the scanner
